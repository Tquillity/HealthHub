# Men's health: evidence-based science

Prostate, testicular and sexual health, male-specific screening and the evidence on popular supplements.

## Prostate

### PSA testing: informed choice
- **Use:** Prostate cancer screening decisions
- **What it is / how it's used:** USPSTF: men 55-69 should make an individual decision about PSA screening after discussing benefits (small reduction in prostate cancer death) and harms (false positives, overdiagnosis, treatment side effects); not recommended at 70+. UK has no national programme but men over 50 can request a PSA test after discussion. Higher risk: Black men and those with a family history.
- **Evidence:** Proven
- **Safety:** Overdiagnosis and overtreatment are real harms.
- **Sources:** [USPSTF: Prostate cancer screening](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/prostate-cancer-screening), [NHS: Prostate cancer symptoms](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/prostate-cancer/symptoms/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Profile: at 50 (45 for higher risk) show a decision aid; store PSA results in lab results with trend.

### Urinary symptoms (enlarged prostate) self-care
- **Use:** Weak flow, frequency, getting up at night
- **What it is / how it's used:** NICE CG97: bladder training, reduce caffeine/alcohol and evening fluids, urethral milking, pelvic floor exercises for post-micturition dribble; a frequency-volume bladder diary helps assessment.
- **Evidence:** Proven (guideline conservative management)
- **Safety:** See GP for blood in urine, pain, inability to pee (emergency), or new symptoms.
- **Sources:** [NICE CG97 LUTS in men](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg97), [NHS: Enlarged prostate](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/prostate-enlargement/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Tracker: 3-day bladder diary template exportable for the GP.

### Saw palmetto
- **Use:** Enlarged prostate symptoms
- **What it is / how it's used:** Large, well-designed trials found it no better than placebo for urinary symptoms.
- **Evidence:** Disproven / likely harmful (ineffective)
- **Safety:** Mild GI effects; generally safe.
- **Sources:** [NCCIH: Saw palmetto](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/saw-palmetto)
- **HealthHub fit:** Myth-check card.

## Testicular and sexual health

### Testicular self-awareness
- **Use:** Early detection of testicular cancer (most common in men 15-49)
- **What it is / how it's used:** Know what is normal; check monthly after a warm shower; see a GP for a painless lump, swelling, heaviness or change in shape. (USPSTF does not recommend clinician screening of asymptomatic men, but symptoms should be checked promptly.)
- **Evidence:** Proven (prompt assessment of symptoms); routine self-exam itself is unproven
- **Safety:** Sudden severe testicular pain is an emergency (torsion).
- **Sources:** [NHS: Testicular cancer symptoms](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/testicular-cancer/symptoms/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Monthly optional routine reminder for teens/men in the household.

### Erectile dysfunction as a heart-health signal
- **Use:** ED
- **What it is / how it's used:** ED often reflects vascular disease, diabetes or high BP; GP checks BP, glucose, cholesterol. Lifestyle: weight loss, exercise, stop smoking, less alcohol. PDE5 inhibitors (sildenafil) available, in the UK also from pharmacies.
- **Evidence:** Proven
- **Safety:** Never combine PDE5 inhibitors with nitrates; avoid unregulated online "herbal Viagra" (often adulterated).
- **Sources:** [NHS: Erectile dysfunction](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/erectile-dysfunction/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Suggest a cardio-metabolic check (BP, HbA1c, lipids) when ED is logged.

### "Male menopause" and testosterone
- **Use:** Fatigue, low libido, low mood in midlife
- **What it is / how it's used:** NHS: true late-onset hypogonadism is uncommon; symptoms are more often due to lifestyle, stress, depression, poor sleep. Diagnosis needs morning blood tests on two occasions. Testosterone therapy only for confirmed deficiency.
- **Evidence:** Proven
- **Safety:** Unsupervised testosterone causes infertility, polycythaemia, and other harms.
- **Sources:** [NHS: The 'male menopause'](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/male-menopause/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Journal: link libido/energy ratings with sleep, alcohol and activity before seeking tests.

### Pelvic floor exercises for men
- **Use:** Dribbling after peeing, incontinence after prostate surgery
- **What it is / how it's used:** NICE recommends supervised pelvic floor muscle training for men with stress incontinence after prostatectomy and for post-micturition dribble.
- **Evidence:** Proven
- **Safety:** Low risk
- **Sources:** [NICE CG97 LUTS in men](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg97), [NHS: Urinary incontinence](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/urinary-incontinence/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Same pelvic-floor routine as women's module, male version.

## Screening

### Abdominal aortic aneurysm (AAA) screening
- **Use:** Detecting a silent, potentially fatal aneurysm
- **What it is / how it's used:** NHS invites all men in the year they turn 65 for a one-off ultrasound; USPSTF: one-time screen for men 65-75 who have ever smoked.
- **Evidence:** Proven
- **Safety:** Low risk
- **Sources:** [NHS: AAA screening](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/abdominal-aortic-aneurysm-screening/), [USPSTF: AAA screening](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/abdominal-aortic-aneurysm-screening)
- **HealthHub fit:** Household screening calendar: auto-reminder at 65.

### Mental health and help-seeking in men
- **Use:** Depression and suicide risk (men account for about three-quarters of UK suicides)
- **What it is / how it's used:** Men often present with irritability, anger, alcohol use or physical complaints rather than sadness; screening and talking therapies are effective.
- **Evidence:** Proven
- **Safety:** Crisis signs need urgent help.
- **Sources:** [USPSTF: Depression and suicide risk screening](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/screening-depression-suicide-risk-adults), [NHS: Help for suicidal thoughts](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/feelings-symptoms-behaviours/behaviours/help-for-suicidal-thoughts/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Mood check-in phrasing that includes irritability/anger and alcohol.

## Red flags

### Men's health red flags
- **Use:** When to see a doctor
- **What it is / how it's used:** Blood in urine or semen, unable to pee (emergency), sudden testicular pain (emergency), testicular lump, new urinary symptoms with bone/back pain or weight loss, erection lasting more than 4 hours (emergency).
- **Evidence:** Proven
- **Safety:** n/a
- **Sources:** [NHS: Prostate cancer symptoms](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/prostate-cancer/symptoms/), [NHS: Testicular cancer symptoms](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/testicular-cancer/symptoms/), [NHS: Enlarged prostate](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/prostate-enlargement/)
