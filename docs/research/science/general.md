# General / cross-cutting: evidence-based science

Cross-cutting health behaviours and app-design principles backed by evidence: self-monitoring, habit formation, alcohol and smoking, fatigue and "when to seek help" logic.

## Self-monitoring and behaviour change

### Self-monitoring improves outcomes
- **Use:** Any health goal (BP, weight, activity, asthma)
- **What it is / how it's used:** Self-monitoring combined with support and regular review is a consistent active ingredient in effective programmes: home BP monitoring with support, asthma self-management, DPP-style lifestyle programmes. Monitoring alone without action plans helps less.
- **Evidence:** Proven
- **Safety:** Low risk; obsessive tracking can raise anxiety in some people.
- **Sources:** [Cochrane: asthma self-management education](https://www.cochrane.org/CD001117), [NICE NG136 Hypertension](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng136), [DPP NEJM 2002 (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11832527/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Design principle: every tracker should pair with a goal, a review moment and a suggested action.

### Intensive multi-session behavioural counselling
- **Use:** Diet, activity, weight, alcohol change
- **What it is / how it's used:** USPSTF finds multi-session behavioural interventions (goal-setting, feedback, problem-solving) effective for diet/activity in CVD risk and for reducing unhealthy drinking.
- **Evidence:** Proven
- **Safety:** Low risk
- **Sources:** [USPSTF: Healthy diet and physical activity counselling](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/healthy-diet-and-physical-activity-counseling-adults-with-high-risk-of-cvd), [USPSTF: Unhealthy alcohol use](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/unhealthy-alcohol-use-in-adolescents-and-adults-screening-and-behavioral-counseling-interventions)
- **HealthHub fit:** Programmes as multi-week courses with weekly check-ins, not one-off tips.

## Alcohol and tobacco

### Alcohol: screen and cut down
- **Use:** Reducing alcohol harm
- **What it is / how it's used:** AUDIT-C (3 questions) identifies risky drinking; brief interventions reduce intake. UK guideline max 14 units/week spread out with drink-free days.
- **Evidence:** Proven
- **Safety:** Heavy daily drinkers should not stop abruptly without medical advice (withdrawal seizures).
- **Sources:** [USPSTF: Unhealthy alcohol use](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/unhealthy-alcohol-use-in-adolescents-and-adults-screening-and-behavioral-counseling-interventions), [NHS: Calculating alcohol units](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/alcohol-advice/calculating-alcohol-units/), [NHS Better Health: Drink less](https://www.nhs.uk/better-health/drink-less/)
- **HealthHub fit:** AUDIT-C quick check + drink log with weekly units.

### Smoking cessation: medicine + support, e-cigarettes as a tool
- **Use:** Quitting smoking
- **What it is / how it's used:** Behavioural support plus pharmacotherapy roughly doubles to triples quit rates; Cochrane finds nicotine e-cigarettes more effective than NRT for quitting.
- **Evidence:** Proven
- **Safety:** E-cigarettes are for smokers switching, not non-smokers or young people.
- **Sources:** [USPSTF: Tobacco cessation](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/tobacco-use-in-adults-and-pregnant-women-counseling-and-interventions), [Cochrane: E-cigarettes for smoking cessation](https://www.cochrane.org/CD010216)
- **HealthHub fit:** Quit programme with craving log and savings counter.

## Fatigue and common "something's off" symptoms

### Tiredness: lifestyle first, then tests
- **Use:** Persistent fatigue
- **What it is / how it's used:** Common causes: poor sleep, stress, low mood, alcohol, inactivity, diet. See GP if tired for weeks without reason: they may test for anaemia, thyroid, diabetes, B12, coeliac.
- **Evidence:** Proven
- **Safety:** Fatigue with weight loss, night sweats, breathlessness or bleeding needs prompt review.
- **Sources:** [NHS: Tiredness and fatigue](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/tiredness-and-fatigue/), [NHS: Iron deficiency anaemia](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/iron-deficiency-anaemia/), [NHS: Underactive thyroid](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/underactive-thyroid-hypothyroidism/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Dashboard insight: energy rating correlated with sleep, activity, alcohol and cycle; suggests tests to ask about.

### Dizziness and vertigo
- **Use:** Feeling dizzy or spinning
- **What it is / how it's used:** Often harmless (dehydration, standing up quickly, inner ear). Lie down, drink water, stand up slowly. BPPV vertigo responds to repositioning manoeuvres done by a clinician.
- **Evidence:** Proven
- **Safety:** Dizziness with chest pain, face/arm weakness, speech problems, severe headache, or fainting: emergency.
- **Sources:** [NHS: Dizziness](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dizziness/), [NHS: Vertigo](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/vertigo/), [NHS: Low blood pressure](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/low-blood-pressure-hypotension/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Symptom log with FAST cross-check.

## Supplements and products: general principles

### Evidence-graded supplement use
- **Use:** Deciding which supplements are worth taking
- **What it is / how it's used:** Targeted supplements with evidence: folic acid pre-conception/early pregnancy, vitamin D in winter, B12 for vegans, iron when deficient. Broad multivitamins and antioxidants for disease prevention: insufficient evidence or harmful. Herbal products vary in content and can interact with medicines (e.g. St John's wort with the pill).
- **Evidence:** Proven (the targeted uses); Disproven / likely harmful (broad prevention claims)
- **Safety:** Always check interactions with prescribed medicines.
- **Sources:** [NHS: Vitamins and minerals](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/vitamins-and-minerals/), [USPSTF: Vitamin supplementation](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/vitamin-supplementation-to-prevent-cvd-and-cancer-preventive-medication), [NCCIH: St John's wort](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/st-johns-wort)
- **HealthHub fit:** Supplement/medication log that shows an evidence badge and interaction warnings for each item.

### Antibiotics: usually not needed for self-limiting infections
- **Use:** Colds, coughs, sore throats, ear infections
- **What it is / how it's used:** NICE guidance and Cochrane reviews: most respiratory and ear infections clear without antibiotics; expected duration: ear infection ~3 days, sore throat ~1 week, cold ~1.5 weeks, cough ~3 weeks.
- **Evidence:** Proven
- **Safety:** Red flags still apply.
- **Sources:** [NICE NG120 Acute cough](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng120/chapter/summary-of-the-evidence), [Cochrane: Antibiotics for acute otitis media](https://www.cochrane.org/CD000219)
- **HealthHub fit:** Illness log shows "typical duration" bar so users know what's normal.

## Where to get help

### Local health services as the default safety net
- **Use:** Knowing where to turn
- **What it is / how it's used:** Sweden: 1177 (phone and 1177.se) for advice, 112 emergencies; UK: NHS 111, pharmacist first for minor illness, 999 emergencies. Pharmacists can treat many minor conditions.
- **Evidence:** Proven (service pathway)
- **Safety:** n/a
- **Sources:** [1177](https://www.1177.se/), [1177: Psykiska sjukdomar och besvär](https://www.1177.se/sjukdomar--besvar/psykiska-sjukdomar-och-besvar/), [NHS: Sore throat (pharmacy first)](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/sore-throat/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Profile setting for country -> every red-flag message shows correct local numbers.

### Unified red-flag engine
- **Use:** Surfacing "see a doctor" signals across all trackers
- **What it is / how it's used:** Common thresholds from guidelines: symptoms lasting 3+ weeks (cough, bowel change, ulcer, hoarseness), unexplained weight loss, blood where it shouldn't be, FAST signs, chest pain, non-blanching rash, suicidal thoughts, BP 180/120+, child fever thresholds by age.
- **Evidence:** Proven (assembled from NHS/NICE criteria)
- **Safety:** App must not diagnose; it should signpost.
- **Sources:** [NHS: Bowel cancer symptoms](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/bowel-cancer/symptoms/), [NHS: Stroke symptoms](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/stroke/symptoms/), [NHS: Meningitis symptoms](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/meningitis/symptoms/), [NHS: Help for suicidal thoughts](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/feelings-symptoms-behaviours/behaviours/help-for-suicidal-thoughts/)
- **HealthHub fit:** A central rules module that every journal/tracker entry passes through; one of the most valuable safety features to build.
