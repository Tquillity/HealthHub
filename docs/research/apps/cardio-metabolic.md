# Cardio-metabolic: apps and tools

Open-source weight and glucose tools that show how family sharing and privacy-first design can work for vitals.

## Weight and body composition

### openScale
- **Use:** Weight and body-composition tracking with Bluetooth scales.
- **What it is:** Open-source Android app (GPL-3.0), free on F-Droid; Play Store open beta.
- **Standout features:** Many Bluetooth scales supported (Beurer, Medisana, Xiaomi, Yunmai...), manual entry otherwise; BMI, body fat, water, muscle, waist and other measurements, custom metrics; goals; CSV import/export; multiple users; assisted weighing for babies or pets.
- **Evidence / credibility:** Consumer tool; body-fat from bathroom scales is approximate.
- **Privacy notes:** No internet permission at all; no cloud, no ads. Optional separate sync app.
- **Sources:** [openScale on GitHub](https://github.com/oliexdev/openScale)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** Multi-user vitals in one household with CSV import/export, and "assisted weighing" (weigh yourself holding the baby, subtract).

## Blood glucose

### Nightscout
- **Use:** Remote viewing and sharing of continuous glucose monitor (CGM) data.
- **What it is:** Free open-source, self-hosted "CGM in the Cloud" project; or paid managed hosting.
- **Standout features:** Uploader app sends CGM data to your own site; any browser can view it; "followers" (parents, watches) see a child's glucose in near real time.
- **Evidence / credibility:** Community DIY tool, not approved or regulated for treatment decisions; docs say not to rely on it alone.
- **Privacy notes:** You host your own data.
- **Sources:** [Nightscout documentation](https://nightscout.github.io/)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** The "follower" model for household spaces: a parent can follow a child's or elderly parent's chosen metrics read-only, with alerts.
