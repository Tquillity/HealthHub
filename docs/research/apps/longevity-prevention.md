# Longevity and prevention: apps and tools

Consumer blood-testing and biomarker services, including Swedish Werlabs.

## Blood-test memberships

### Werlabs (Swedish)
- **Use:** Direct-to-consumer blood tests in Sweden.
- **What it is:** Swedish service; single tests (thyroid, vitamins, hormones) and health checks such as Kvinna/Man (SEK 2,395) and Kvinna/Man Plus (SEK 3,995), with member prices.
- **Standout features:** 200+ sampling sites; samples analysed by Synlab, Unilabs or Karolinska lab; a Werlabs doctor comments on results within 2-4 working days; results stored in a personal "Werlabs-journal"; "health age" on health checks; menopause midwife consultation in Kvinna Plus (40-60).
- **Evidence / credibility:** Accredited labs, licensed doctors (50+). Population screening without symptoms has limited evidence for many markers.
- **Privacy notes:** Swedish healthcare data rules.
- **Sources:** [Werlabs](https://www.werlabs.se/)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** A lab results section where users type in (or upload a PDF of) their Werlabs/1177 results, plotted over time per marker with the lab's reference range and the doctor's comment stored next to it.

### Function Health
- **Use:** Broad biomarker testing membership (US).
- **What it is:** $365/year (family add-on $340), 160+ tests, Quest labs or home draw.
- **Standout features:** Two tests a year; clinician notes organised by body system; action plan that updates with new results; sharing with your doctor; AI chat over your own data.
- **Evidence / credibility:** CLIA labs; the value of testing 100+ markers in healthy people is debated.
- **Privacy notes:** Results can be connected to AI tools; read terms.
- **Sources:** [Function Health](https://www.functionhealth.com/)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** Group lab results by body system (heart, thyroid, iron) with a short "next step" note per group.

### Superpower
- **Use:** Biomarker membership (US).
- **What it is:** $199/year, 100+ biomarkers in one draw; optional DNA bundle.
- **Standout features:** Upload past labs; wearable sync (Apple Health, Whoop, Oura); trend per marker; biological age and 17 health scores; clinician care team.
- **Evidence / credibility:** "Biological age" scores are not standardised.
- **Privacy notes:** Combines labs, DNA and wearables; high sensitivity.
- **Sources:** [Superpower](https://superpower.com/)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** "Upload past labs" so history starts on day one.

### InsideTracker
- **Use:** Blood-test based nutrition and performance guidance.
- **What it is:** US subscription service.
- **Standout features:** "Optimize wellness and performance through blood tests, nutrition and science" (details not captured: homepage gave only a tagline).
- **Evidence / credibility:** Not assessed.
- **Privacy notes:** Not assessed.
- **Sources:** [InsideTracker](https://www.insidetracker.com/)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** Link lab gaps (e.g. low ferritin) to recipes rich in that nutrient.
