# Pain and musculoskeletal: apps and tools

Headache and pain diaries. See also Bearable and Guava in [general.md](general.md) for body-map symptom logging.

## Headache and migraine

### Migraine Buddy
- **Use:** Migraine and headache diary.
- **What it is:** iOS/Android, free with MBplus subscription (detailed reports, advanced features).
- **Standout features:** Logs start/end, pain intensity and location, triggers, symptoms, medication and relief methods; weather and sleep factors; AI-suggested triggers; summary report to share with a doctor showing frequency and how well each medication worked.
- **Evidence / credibility:** Self-management tool, not a medical device. Doximity's review notes diet triggers are not tracked directly.
- **Privacy notes:** Cloud account; health data also used for research per company model, check settings.
- **Sources:** [Doximity app of the week](https://opmed.doximity.com/articles/app-of-the-week-migraine-buddy), [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id975074413)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** A headache log with "did the remedy help?" follow-up 2 hours later, linked to journal, sleep and cycle data, and a doctor report with medication effectiveness. HealthHub can do the diet link Migraine Buddy lacks, via meals and recipes.
