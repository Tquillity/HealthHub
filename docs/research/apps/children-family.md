# Children and family: apps and tools

Baby trackers built for several caregivers, and the Swedish public and private care apps a Swedish household already uses.

## Baby and toddler tracking

### Huckleberry
- **Use:** Baby sleep, feeding and diaper tracking.
- **What it is:** iOS/Android, free tracking with Plus/Premium subscriptions.
- **Standout features:** SweetSpot nap and bedtime predictor (from 2 months): starts from age-based wake windows and adapts to logged naps; a single odd day does not reset the schedule; pediatric sleep specialist input.
- **Evidence / credibility:** Wake windows are standard guidance; predictor accuracy is company-claimed and reviews are mixed.
- **Privacy notes:** Cloud sync across caregivers.
- **Sources:** [Huckleberry: SweetSpot](https://huckleberrycare.com/blog/how-to-use-our-magical-sleep-predictor-sweetspot), [Huckleberry Plus FAQ](https://huckleberry.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500007717641-What-is-Huckleberry-Plus), [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/huckleberry-baby-tracker/id1169136078)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** A child profile in the household space with nap/feed/diaper quick-log and a "next nap around" hint.

### Shared-care baby trackers (Nara, Baby Tracker, Pebbi)
- **Use:** Coordinating feeds, naps and diapers between parents, grandparents and nannies.
- **What it is:** Several iOS/Android apps; Nara is free and ad-free; Baby Tracker has a "Group Plus" where one subscription covers the whole sync group.
- **Standout features:** Real-time sync between caregivers; Pebbi's "handover" view shows everything since you last checked, useful for night shifts.
- **Evidence / credibility:** Consumer tools.
- **Privacy notes:** Cloud sync; check each app.
- **Sources:** [Nara Baby Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1444639029), [Pebbi Shared Care Baby Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6756668942), [Baby Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/app/id779656557)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** A "since you last looked" handover card in household spaces (who logged what, for whom), and one household subscription instead of per-person.

## Swedish healthcare apps

### 1177 (Vårdguiden)
- **Use:** Swedish public healthcare portal: medical records, prescriptions, bookings, nurse advice.
- **What it is:** Free app run by Inera AB (owned by regions, municipalities and SKR); BankID or Freja eID+ login.
- **Standout features:** Read your journal (medical record), view and renew prescriptions, book/cancel appointments, high-cost protection status, call 1177 nurse line 24/7. Parents can act for children under 13.
- **Evidence / credibility:** Official national service.
- **Privacy notes:** Strong e-ID authentication; data held by regions.
- **Sources:** [1177: about the app](https://www.1177.se/om-1177/om-1177-appen/), [Freja: child access from 13](https://frejaeid.com/en/access-to-1177-se-for-children-over-the-age-of-13/)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** Mirror the age model: parents manage child profiles until a set age, then the child gets their own login and controls sharing. Deep-link to 1177 for anything medical ("call 1177 if...").

### Kry
- **Use:** Video/chat doctor visits plus physical clinics in Sweden.
- **What it is:** Stockholm-based provider; BankID login; patient fee set by region (about SEK 275; free for children per a comparison site).
- **Standout features:** Pre-visit symptom questionnaire with photo upload; prescriptions and referrals; walk-in clinics.
- **Evidence / credibility:** Licensed healthcare provider.
- **Privacy notes:** Patient records under Swedish healthcare law.
- **Sources:** [Kry](https://kry.se/en/), [Kry on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=se.kry.android)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** A "prepare for a visit" summary: recent symptoms, photos (rash, wound), meds and vitals in one screen to read out or upload.

### Doktor24
- **Use:** Digital and physical primary care in Sweden.
- **What it is:** Private provider since 2016; chat first, then video; also phone visits for older people without smartphones; vaccinations at pharmacies.
- **Standout features:** 24/7 access; drop-in queue or booked time.
- **Evidence / credibility:** Licensed provider.
- **Privacy notes:** Swedish healthcare law applies.
- **Sources:** [Doktor24 on App Store (SE)](https://apps.apple.com/se/app/doktor24-%C3%B6ppet-livet-runt/id1249339459), [Läkartidningen on phone service](https://lakartidningen.se/nyheter/doktor-24-erbjuder-app-lost-via-telefon/)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** Keep links to the household's chosen care providers in each profile, plus an elderly-friendly simplified view.
