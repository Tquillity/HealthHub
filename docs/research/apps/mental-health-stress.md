# Mental health and stress: apps and tools

Meditation, mood journaling and self-care apps relevant to HealthHub's journal and routines.

## Mood journaling

### Daylio
- **Use:** Quick mood and activity diary.
- **What it is:** iOS/Android app, free with Premium (advanced stats, auto backups, more icons, PIN lock).
- **Standout features:** Five-point mood plus tap-to-select activity icons, optional note; monthly mood chart; best weekday; which activities go with good moods.
- **Evidence / credibility:** Wellness tool, no clinical claims.
- **Privacy notes:** Data stored locally on the phone, with optional backups to the user's own cloud storage; reviewers note backups are not encrypted.
- **Sources:** [Wikipedia: Daylio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daylio), [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1194023242)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** A "two-tap" journal entry: pick mood, tap activity icons (walk, family, cooked, alcohol), note optional. Rich structured data with almost no effort.

### How We Feel
- **Use:** Emotion check-ins and regulation strategies.
- **What it is:** Free nonprofit app (How We Feel Project) built with Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence researchers; no ads or subscription.
- **Standout features:** Colour-coded emotion grid (energy x pleasantness, from the RULER "Mood Meter"); large emotion vocabulary; trends, optionally alongside sleep and exercise from HealthKit; short strategy videos.
- **Evidence / credibility:** Based on established emotional-intelligence research; 2022 App Store Award (Cultural Impact).
- **Privacy notes:** Donation funded; no ad model.
- **Sources:** [Yale School of Medicine article](https://medicine.yale.edu/news-article/the-how-we-feel-app-helping-emotions-work-for-us-not-against-us/), [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1562706384)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** The 2D mood grid (energy vs pleasantness) as the journal mood picker; it gives better data than a single 1-5 scale and helps kids name feelings too.

## Self-care and habits

### Finch
- **Use:** Gentle self-care goals with a virtual pet.
- **What it is:** iOS/Android, freemium.
- **Standout features:** Pet bird grows as you complete small goals; daily reflection prompts; breathing exercises; no punishment for missed days.
- **Evidence / credibility:** No clinical claims.
- **Privacy notes:** Mozilla notes little personal information is required.
- **Sources:** [Engadget review](https://www.engadget.com/apps/this-self-care-virtual-pet-is-helping-me-get-my-act-together-160027169.html), [Mozilla Privacy Not Included: Finch](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/es/privacynotincluded/finch-self-care-pet/), [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/1528595748)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** Compassionate streak design: celebrate progress, never shame a broken streak. A shared household "pet" or garden that grows with everyone's routines could suit kids.

## Meditation and mindfulness

### Headspace
- **Use:** Guided meditation, sleep, and (in some markets) coaching/therapy.
- **What it is:** Subscription app (iOS/Android/web).
- **Standout features:** Structured courses, sleepcasts, short "SOS" sessions.
- **Evidence / credibility:** Publishes its own research and white papers; its science page refers to Headspace-specific studies but does not list figures in text.
- **Privacy notes:** Account-based cloud service.
- **Sources:** [Headspace science](https://www.headspace.com/science)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** Short guided routines (breathing, body scan) as steps inside HealthHub routines.

### Calm
- **Use:** Meditation, sleep stories, relaxation.
- **What it is:** Subscription app; Calm Health for employers/health plans.
- **Standout features:** Sleep stories, soundscapes, daily calm sessions, mood check-in.
- **Evidence / credibility:** Lists RCTs including a pragmatic workplace RCT and a PLOS ONE RCT on sleep-related symptoms in adults with sleep disturbance.
- **Privacy notes:** Account-based cloud service.
- **Sources:** [Calm Health clinical studies](https://health.calm.com/resources/clinical-studies/)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** A mood check-in before and after a routine to show its effect.

### Insight Timer
- **Use:** Free meditation library.
- **What it is:** iOS/Android/web, free with optional premium.
- **Standout features:** Markets "the largest free library in the world", no ads; teacher directory; live events.
- **Evidence / credibility:** Content quality varies by teacher.
- **Privacy notes:** Account-based.
- **Sources:** [Insight Timer](https://insighttimer.com/)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** A plain meditation timer with interval bells, reusing the existing PomoZen timer.

## Chatbots

### Woebot (discontinued consumer app)
- **Use:** CBT-style mental-health chatbot.
- **What it is:** Consumer app retired 30 June 2025; still offered via partner organisations.
- **Standout features:** Daily conversational check-ins teaching CBT skills.
- **Evidence / credibility:** Founder said the shutdown was driven by the cost and difficulty of FDA marketing authorisation, not clinical failure.
- **Privacy notes:** Users could download conversation history before shutdown; data anonymised by 31 July 2025.
- **Sources:** [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2025/07/02/woebot-mental-health-chatbot-shuts-down-founder-says-ai-moving-faster-than-regulators/), [MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/woebot-health-shutting-down-its-app)
- **What HealthHub could borrow:** Caution: AI "therapy" features invite regulation. If any AI is added, keep it to summarising the user's own logs, and always offer a full data export.
