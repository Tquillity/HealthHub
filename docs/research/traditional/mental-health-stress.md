# Mental health and stress: traditional and folk remedies

Calming herbs from European folk medicine, with the important medication warnings.

## Low mood

### St John's wort (johannesört)
- **Use:** Low mood, mild depression.
- **What it is / how it's used:** European folk herb, traditionally picked at midsummer (St John's Day). Tea or standardised tablets.
- **Evidence:** Promising for mild to moderate depression (see the science lens). Here we focus on safety.
- **Safety:** **Dangerous interactions: weakens contraceptive pills, warfarin, transplant, HIV, cancer, heart and seizure drugs, and statins. Serotonin syndrome risk with antidepressants. Photosensitivity. May be unsafe in pregnancy.**
- **Sources:** [NCCIH: St. John's wort](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/st-johns-wort)

## Anxiety and stress

### Chamomile tea
- **Use:** Anxiety, stress.
- **What it is / how it's used:** European and Latin American calming tea.
- **Evidence:** Traditional / anecdotal to Promising. A 2019 meta-analysis found no effect on anxiety. A 2024 systematic review was more positive but did not pool its data.
- **Safety:** Daisy-family allergy.
- **Sources:** [Hieu 2019 meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31006899/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Wind-down journal prompt.

### Lavender
- **Use:** Anxiety, restlessness.
- **What it is / how it's used:** Mediterranean tradition. Sachets and oil. Oral lavender oil capsules today.
- **Evidence:** Promising (oral lavender oil for anxiety in small studies).
- **Safety:** Stomach upset and burping (oral). Skin allergy (topical).
- **Sources:** [NCCIH: Lavender](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/lavender)
- **HealthHub fit:** Stress routine.

### Valerian
- **Use:** Nervousness, sleep.
- **What it is / how it's used:** European root tea.
- **Evidence:** Traditional / anecdotal (inconsistent).
- **Safety:** Avoid with alcohol and sedatives.
- **Sources:** [NCCIH: Valerian](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/valerian)
