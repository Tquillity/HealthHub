# Sleep: traditional and folk remedies

Bedtime teas, herbs and rituals passed down in European and other households.

## Trouble falling or staying asleep

### Chamomile tea (kamomillte)
- **Use:** Falling asleep, night waking.
- **What it is / how it's used:** European, Egyptian and Latin American (manzanilla) bedtime tea. 1-2 cups in the evening.
- **Evidence:** Promising. A 2024 meta-analysis (10 studies, 772 people) found improved sleep quality, mainly fewer night awakenings, but no change in total sleep time.
- **Safety:** Daisy-family or ragweed allergy. Theoretical interaction with warfarin and sedatives.
- **Sources:** [2024 chamomile sleep meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39106912/), [Hieu 2019 meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31006899/), [Sleep Foundation: chamomile tea](https://www.sleepfoundation.org/sleep-aids/does-chamomile-tea-make-you-sleep)
- **HealthHub fit:** Bedtime routine item. Sleep journal correlation ("nights with chamomile").

### Valerian (vänderot)
- **Use:** Insomnia, restlessness.
- **What it is / how it's used:** European folk herb used since ancient Greece and in Nordic folk medicine. Root tea or tablets (300-600 mg).
- **Evidence:** Traditional / anecdotal (inconsistent). The American Academy of Sleep Medicine recommends against it for chronic insomnia.
- **Safety:** Short-term use appears safe. Headache, stomach upset, vivid dreams. **Don't combine with alcohol or sedatives.** Unknown safety in pregnancy.
- **Sources:** [NCCIH: Valerian](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/valerian)
- **HealthHub fit:** Remedy library with a sedative-interaction warning.

### Lavender (lavendel) pillow or oil
- **Use:** Sleep, calming.
- **What it is / how it's used:** Provençal and Mediterranean tradition. Lavender sachets under the pillow, or a few drops of oil.
- **Evidence:** Traditional / anecdotal for sleep. Oral lavender oil shows some benefit for anxiety. Aromatherapy evidence is preliminary.
- **Safety:** Skin allergy. A few cases of breast swelling in children using topical lavender products (unclear whether it was the cause).
- **Sources:** [NCCIH: Lavender](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/lavender)
- **HealthHub fit:** Wind-down routine.

### Evening sauna (kvällsbastu)
- **Use:** Relaxation, better sleep.
- **What it is / how it's used:** Finnish and Swedish evening sauna habit.
- **Evidence:** Traditional / anecdotal for sleep specifically. It is associated with many cardiovascular benefits (Finnish cohorts).
- **Safety:** Avoid with alcohol or acute illness. Drink fluids.
- **Sources:** [Laukkanen 2018 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30077204/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Routine plus a sleep-quality correlation in the journal.

### Warm milk with honey
- **Use:** Falling asleep.
- **What it is / how it's used:** A Nordic and European bedtime ritual: a cup of warm milk, often with honey.
- **Evidence:** Traditional / anecdotal. It works mainly as a calming ritual. The honey evidence covers cough-related sleep loss in children.
- **Safety:** No honey under 1 year. Brush teeth afterwards.
- **Sources:** [Cochrane: Honey for acute cough in children (sleep outcome)](https://www.cochrane.org/CD007094/ARI_honey-childrens-coughs)
- **HealthHub fit:** Bedtime routine recipe.
