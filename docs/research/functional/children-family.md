# Children & family: functional and integrative approaches

Integrative and alternative remedies families commonly use for children, with the specific safety issues for kids.

## Remedies to avoid

### Homeopathic teething tablets and gels
- **Use:** Teething pain.
- **What it is / how it's used:** Tablets or gels marketed as "natural".
- **Evidence:** Disproven / likely harmful. Homeopathy has no reliable evidence for any condition. The FDA has warned about teething tablets containing too much belladonna.
- **Safety:** Avoid. Use a cold teething ring, or ask a pharmacist about suitable pain relief.
- **Sources:** [NCCIH: Homeopathy](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/homeopathy)
- **HealthHub fit:** Household/child profile: a teething remedy card with safe options and a warning.

### Chiropractic adjustments for infants and children
- **Use:** Colic, ear infections, asthma.
- **What it is / how it's used:** Spinal adjustments.
- **Evidence:** Disproven / likely harmful. No high-quality study shows benefit for non-musculoskeletal conditions.
- **Safety:** Delays proper care.
- **Sources:** [NCCIH: Spinal Manipulation](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/spinal-manipulation-what-you-need-to-know)
- **HealthHub fit:** Remedy library warning card in the children section.

### Functional "child health checks" with food-antibody panels
- **Use:** Find hidden causes of stomach aches, eczema, behaviour issues.
- **What it is / how it's used:** Private clinics order broad panels. One documented Swedish case was a 30,000 SEK check with 44 food-antibody tests.
- **Evidence:** Disproven / likely harmful. IgG food tests are not valid, and positive results are normal in healthy children.
- **Safety:** Unnecessary diets can harm growth and nutrition, and real conditions can be missed.
- **Sources:** [VoF/Folkvett: Funktionell medicin (PDF)](https://www.vof.se/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/5-funktionll-medicin.pdf), [CSACI position statement on IgG testing](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3443017/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Household: a symptom diary for a child that can be shared with BVC or a GP instead.

### Unpasteurised juices and juice cleanses for children
- **Use:** "Detox" or "immune" juices.
- **What it is / how it's used:** Raw cold-pressed juices.
- **Evidence:** Disproven / likely harmful.
- **Safety:** Unpasteurised juice can cause serious illness in children.
- **Sources:** [NCCIH: Detoxes and Cleanses](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/detoxes-and-cleanses-what-you-need-to-know)
- **HealthHub fit:** Recipes: flag raw juices as "not for small children".

## Remedies with child-specific cautions

### Melatonin for children
- **Use:** Bedtime problems.
- **What it is / how it's used:** Low-dose melatonin (prescription in Sweden).
- **Evidence:** Promising for specific sleep disorders. Long-term effects on puberty and development are unknown.
- **Safety:** About 11,000 US emergency visits (2019-2022) from accidental ingestion by children 5 and under, mostly gummies. Gummy doses are often wrong.
- **Sources:** [NCCIH: Melatonin](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/melatonin-what-you-need-to-know)
- **HealthHub fit:** Household: child sleep routine first (wind-down, light), plus a medicine-cabinet safety reminder.

### Probiotics in premature or very ill infants
- **Use:** Gut health.
- **What it is / how it's used:** Probiotic drops.
- **Evidence:** n/a (safety entry).
- **Safety:** Disproven / likely harmful outside medical supervision: severe or fatal infections have occurred in premature infants, and the FDA has warned about this.
- **Sources:** [NCCIH: Probiotics](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/probiotics-what-you-need-to-know)
- **HealthHub fit:** Child profile: warning when a probiotic is logged for an infant under 3 months or one born preterm.

### Lavender and tea-tree products on children
- **Use:** Calming, sleep, skin.
- **What it is / how it's used:** Essential oils, soaps, lotions.
- **Evidence:** Traditional / anecdotal.
- **Safety:** Breast-tissue swelling has been reported in children using lavender products (cause unclear).
- **Sources:** [NCCIH: Sleep Disorders In Depth](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/sleep-disorders-in-depth)
- **HealthHub fit:** Remedy library: "adults only" flag.

### Honey for cough (over 1 year)
- **Use:** Night-time cough.
- **What it is / how it's used:** 1/2 to 1 teaspoon before bed.
- **Evidence:** Proven (low-to-moderate certainty; Cochrane).
- **Safety:** Never under 12 months (botulism).
- **Sources:** [Cochrane: Honey for acute cough in children](https://www.cochrane.org/CD007094/ARI_honey-for-acute-cough-in-children)
