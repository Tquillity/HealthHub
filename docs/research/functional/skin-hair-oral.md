# Skin, hair & oral: functional and integrative approaches

Light-based "biohacking" devices for skin and hair, as sold for home use.

## Light therapy

### Red / near-infrared light (photobiomodulation) for hair loss
- **Use:** Androgenetic alopecia (pattern hair loss).
- **What it is / how it's used:** Laser caps, helmets or combs used several times a week for months.
- **Evidence:** Promising. RCTs show some regrowth and thicker hair, and one study found results comparable to minoxidil. It will not restore a full head of hair, and independent trials are still needed.
- **Safety:** Low risk. Protect your eyes. Devices vary widely, and "FDA-registered" means little (unlike "FDA-cleared").
- **Sources:** [AAD: Is red light therapy right for your skin?](https://aad.org/public/cosmetic/safety/red-light-therapy), [NPR: Can red light therapy really deliver?](https://www.npr.org/2026/04/13/nx-s1-5759287/is-red-light-therapy-healthy-or-all-hype), [Skeptoid: red light therapy](https://skeptoid.com/episodes/1044)
- **HealthHub fit:** Routine + photo journal: a monthly hair photo comparison for anyone trying a device.

### Red light therapy for wrinkles and acne
- **Use:** Fine lines, acne, skin "glow".
- **What it is / how it's used:** LED masks or panels, 10-20 minutes several times a week.
- **Evidence:** Promising (modest). Strongest for acne; modest for fine lines, and below retinoids or professional lasers. A 2026 review says consumer marketing often exceeds the evidence, and home devices may be weaker than clinic protocols.
- **Safety:** Low risk. Eye protection. Caution with light-sensitising drugs.
- **Sources:** [AAD: Red light therapy](https://aad.org/public/cosmetic/safety/red-light-therapy), [JDD: Red light therapy in cosmetic dermatology: marketing claims vs evidence](https://jddonline.com/articles/red-light-therapy-in-cosmetic-dermatology-marketing-claims-backed-evidence-S1545961626P9844X/), [Photobiomodulation clinical applications review (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33471046/)
- **HealthHub fit:** Routine timer for device sessions, plus a skin photo journal.

### Red light therapy for wound healing and other clinical uses
- **Use:** Slow-healing wounds, radiation dermatitis, neuropathy.
- **What it is / how it's used:** Clinic-based photobiomodulation.
- **Evidence:** Promising. A 2025 expert consensus supports these clinical uses.
- **Safety:** Low risk when used under care.
- **Sources:** [NPR: Can red light therapy really deliver?](https://www.npr.org/2026/04/13/nx-s1-5759287/is-red-light-therapy-healthy-or-all-hype)
