# Men's health: functional and integrative approaches

Herbal prostate remedies commonly recommended in integrative care.

## Prostate

### Saw palmetto for enlarged prostate (BPH)
- **Use:** Urinary symptoms from benign prostatic hyperplasia.
- **What it is / how it's used:** Berry extract, typically 320 mg/day.
- **Evidence:** Disproven / likely harmful (ineffective). A 2023 review of 27 studies found that saw palmetto alone gives little or no benefit for BPH symptoms.
- **Safety:** Well tolerated (up to 3 years in studies); does not affect PSA readings. The risk is delaying assessment of urinary symptoms.
- **Sources:** [NCCIH: Saw Palmetto](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/saw-palmetto)
- **HealthHub fit:** Remedy library evidence note; a urinary symptom score (e.g. night-time toilet trips) in vitals to bring to a GP.
